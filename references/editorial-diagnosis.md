# Editorial diagnosis

Classify the article before proposing visuals.

Primary archetypes: product reveal, how-to, personal narrative, opinion essay, research brief, news analysis, interview/dialogue, and collection/recommendation. A piece may have a secondary archetype; proposals should explore that tension.

Return a compact diagnosis:

```json
{
  "primary_archetype": "product reveal",
  "secondary_archetype": "personal narrative",
  "reader_promise": "what the reader gets",
  "voice": ["first-person", "practical", "restrained"],
  "emotional_curve": ["friction", "discovery", "proof", "invitation"],
  "density": "medium",
  "evidence": ["real screenshot", "workflow", "known limitation"],
  "visual_moments": ["problem comparison", "interaction", "learning loop"],
  "preserve": ["phrasing and structural invariants"]
}
```

Before proposing, check recent publication memory. Same brand does not imply same color or Hero. Do not default to Hero + numbered headings + cards.
