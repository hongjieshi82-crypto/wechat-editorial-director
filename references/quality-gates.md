# Quality gates

## Source integrity

Title is not duplicated in the body. Facts, caveats, links, names, code, quotations, and author voice are preserved.

## Editorial coherence

The whole article follows the chosen direction. Opening, middle, and ending do not resemble unrelated templates. Color is not the main source of variation. Repetition is justified by content.

## Visual integrity

Every image has a documented job and accurate caption. Sources resolve. Screenshots and evidence are real, data verified, rights requirements recorded, and files appropriately compressed.

Every candidate image was visually inspected. Generated images are saved in the article project, not left only in a generator cache. A schematic is labeled as a schematic; a generated UI must never be presented as a real screenshot.

## WeChat safety

Inside exported article content reject `style`, `script`, `iframe`, `object`, `embed`, forms, classes, IDs, CSS variables, media queries, animation, and positioning. Avoid layouts dependent on grid, hover, fixed width, or scrolling. Final styles are inline.

## Mobile inspection

Render at 375px. Check headline wrapping, body size and contrast, long paragraphs, SVG/image scaling, captions, overflow, dark-mode-sensitive colors, and clean copied payload. Deliver with zero known blocking errors.

For revisions, verify the new version visibly differs in the requested area. Use a new filename or explicit cache-busting revision. Test the copy button after its label or payload changes. A self-contained copy deliverable has no unresolved local or remote image references.

## Cover inspection

Verify the wide cover and exact center-square crop separately. The main title, face, product, and interaction cue must remain inside the square-safe zone. Deliver a safe-area proof image; do not claim crop safety from visual intuition alone. Generated backgrounds contain no accidental text or logos, and final Chinese typography is applied deterministically.
