# Quality gates

## Source integrity

Title is not duplicated in the body. Facts, caveats, links, names, code, quotations, and author voice are preserved.

Read the complete final body continuously once before packaging. Fix sentence fragments, missing transitions, ambiguous pronouns, stale future tense, accidental repetition, abrupt register changes, and slogans isolated only for visual effect. Preserve intentional colloquial phrasing and humor; do not polish the author's voice into corporate prose.

## Editorial coherence

The whole article follows the chosen direction. Opening, middle, and ending do not resemble unrelated templates. Color is not the main source of variation. Repetition is justified by content.

The ending reads as a natural conclusion, callback, or elevation of the article. Reject a default large dark closing card, slogan poster, boxed "final quote", or isolated oversized final sentence unless the user explicitly requested it. If the final thought completes the preceding paragraph, keep it in continuous prose instead of manufacturing a climax with size, weight, or divider lines.

The opening is derived from the selected direction rather than a reusable dark-card template. Reject a heavy hero that merely repeats the WeChat title/cover or conflicts with a light visual system. A quiet title lead or direct prose opening is valid.

Prominent external destinations use the light webpage-card grammar rather than terminal cosplay or exposed long URLs. Verify the displayed address and actual `href` match. Keep ordinary citations compact so link cards remain meaningful.

## Visual integrity

Every image has a documented job and accurate caption. Sources resolve. Screenshots and evidence are real, data verified, rights requirements recorded, and files appropriately compressed.

Every candidate image was visually inspected. Generated images are saved in the article project, not left only in a generator cache. A schematic is labeled as a schematic; a generated UI must never be presented as a real screenshot.

The issue has a declared source strategy and visual system. The cover and inline images share the approved style anchor, medium, palette behavior, material/line/lighting grammar, and recurring subject policy. Reject decorative filler, mixed illustration styles, evidence replaced by generation, or a cover that belongs to a different visual family from the body.

## WeChat safety

Inside exported article content reject `style`, `script`, `iframe`, `object`, `embed`, forms, classes, IDs, CSS variables, media queries, animation, and positioning. Avoid layouts dependent on grid, hover, fixed width, or scrolling. Final styles are inline.

## Copy-ready inspection

Check body size and contrast, image/SVG responsiveness, dark-mode-sensitive colors, unresolved sources, and the clean copied payload without building a simulated phone container. Do not generate a phone-width screenshot or mobile render by default. Perform mobile rendering only when the user requests it or a concrete responsive defect needs diagnosis.

For revisions, verify the new version visibly differs in the requested area. Use a new filename or explicit cache-busting revision. Test the copy button after its label or payload changes. A self-contained copy deliverable has no unresolved local or remote image references.

## Cover inspection

Verify the wide cover and exact center-square crop separately. The main title, face, product, and interaction cue must remain inside the square-safe zone. Deliver a safe-area proof image; do not claim crop safety from visual intuition alone. Generated backgrounds contain no accidental text or logos. Accept generated Chinese typography only when every character is exact; otherwise composite it deterministically into designed negative space.

Reject a cover that has no v1 diagnosis and refinement pass. Confirm that v2 preserves the successful concept while visibly improving the recorded problems. Check title integration, focal hierarchy, lighting depth, prop grouping, character anatomy, unintended generated text, and recognizable brand marks. Deterministic typography is preferred when generated title text is not exact, but it must use designed negative space rather than a generic overlay box.
